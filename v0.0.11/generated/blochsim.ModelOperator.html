ModelOperator#

class blochsim.ModelOperator[source]#

Bases: Module

A signal model over parameter maps, with its derivatives.

Maps are stacked on the last axis: (..., channels) in, (..., contrasts) out, every leading axis a voxel axis. Only physics() moves the channel axis to the front, as deepinv expects.

Parameters:

  • acquisition (Simulator) – Sequence being inverted, with every property not solved for already bound. A property bound as a map is one value per voxel.

  • *unknown (str) – Names of the properties solved for, in channel order. At least one.

  • bounds (mapping, default=None) – {name: (low, high)} in the property’s units; either end may be None, and None leaves every unknown unbounded. Enforced by solving for a transformed variable, so no iterate leaves the bounds.

  • scale (mapping, default=None) – {name: value}, step scale of an unbounded parameter in its own units; unlisted ones use 1. Bounded parameters are scaled by their interval.

  • amplitude (bool, default=True) – Carry a complex amplitude multiplying the model output, as two extra channels (real, imaginary).

  • subspace (Subspace, default=None) – Basis the prediction is projected onto, to solve in the temporal basis rather than in the contrasts.

Raises:

ValueError – If nothing is unknown, a bound or scale names a property that is not unknown, a bound is not increasing, or a scale is not positive.

Notes

The operator follows the device of the maps it is called with; to() moves nothing.

An equality constraint is imposed by writing the model on the remaining parameters. For fat-water fractions that sum to one, make the fat fraction the only unknown and write water as 1 - f inside the model; the constraint then holds exactly at every iterate.

Examples

operator = ModelOperator(
    MultiEchoSimulator(TE=echo_times, T1=1000.0),
    "T2",
    bounds={"T2": (10.0, 300.0)},
)
maps = operator.initial((128, 128), T2=80.0)
images = operator.A(maps)          # (128, 128, echoes), complex

Wrap the operator as a deepinv.physics.Physics.

The channel axis moves to the front: (batch, channels, *xyz) in, (batch, contrasts, *xyz) out. encoding * operator.physics() composes with an encoding operator.

Parameters:

**kwargs – Passed to deepinv.physics.Physics, e.g. a noise model.

Return type:

deepinv.physics.Physics

Raises:

ImportError – If deepinv is not installed.

Notes

A deepinv ComposedPhysics differentiates the chain by autograd, not by the analytic products of this class. GaussNewton chains the analytic products.
