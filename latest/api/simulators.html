Simulators#

The sequences that ship with BlochSim, as Simulator subclasses. A constructor fixes protocol arguments, the keywords of simulate(); tissue properties are given at the call. See the Signal model page.

Closed form#

Signals that have an analytic expression, evaluated in one pass.

blochsim.simulators.bSSFPSimulator

Balanced SSFP steady state, read at the echo time.

blochsim.simulators.SPGRSimulator

Spoiled gradient-echo steady state, read at the echo time.

blochsim.simulators.MP2RAGESimulator

Two gradient-echo blocks read at two inversion times, in closed form.

blochsim.simulators.InversionRecoverySimulator

Longitudinal recovery from an inversion, read at a series of delays.

blochsim.simulators.MultiEchoSimulator

Transverse decay, read at a series of echo times.

blochsim.simulators.DoubleAngleSimulator

Excitation at a series of flip angles, scaled by the transmit field.

blochsim.simulators.SpinEchoSimulator

Spin echo repeated every TR, optionally behind an inversion.

blochsim.simulators.LookLockerSimulator

Recovery from an inversion under a spoiled train of small flip angles.

blochsim.simulators.MOLLISimulator

Look-Locker readout blocks separated by free recovery, after one inversion.

blochsim.simulators.IRbSSFPSimulator

Recovery from an inversion under a balanced SSFP train.

blochsim.simulators.HSFPSimulator

Balanced train with a slowly varying flip angle, in the hybrid state.

blochsim.simulators.MultiGradientEchoSimulator

Water and fat, dephasing and decaying across a gradient-echo train.

blochsim.simulators.IRMultiGradientEchoSimulator

Multi-gradient-echo train read at a series of inversion times.

blochsim.simulators.DiffusionSimulator

Attenuation by Gaussian diffusion at a series of b-values.

blochsim.simulators.LorentzianSimulator

Z-spectrum as equilibrium minus one Lorentzian line per pool.

blochsim.simulators.ASLSimulator

Label-control difference of arterial spin labelling, over time.

State machine#

Trains that are played out event by event on the extended phase graph engine (Extended phase graphs).

blochsim.simulators.FSESimulator

Refocused echo train, sampled at every echo.

blochsim.simulators.HyperechoSimulator

Refocused train mirrored about a central refocusing pulse.

blochsim.simulators.StimulatedEchoSimulator

Three pulses, sampled at the spin echo of the first two and at the stimulated echo.

blochsim.simulators.MPRAGESimulator

Inversion followed by a spoiled train, sampled at the k-space centre.

blochsim.simulators.MPnRAGESimulator

Inversion followed by a spoiled gradient-echo train, every shot read.

blochsim.simulators.MRFSimulator

Inversion followed by a variable flip-angle train whose readouts wind on.

blochsim.simulators.FLASHSimulator

Spoiled gradient-echo train from equilibrium, optionally inverted first.

blochsim.simulators.TrueFISPSimulator

Balanced SSFP train from equilibrium, optionally inverted first.

blochsim.simulators.fmSSFPSimulator

Balanced SSFP train with RF phase swept through one passband per sweep.

blochsim.simulators.CESTSimulator

Z-spectrum simulated through a train of rectangular saturation pulses.

Functional wrappers#

The *_sim functions evaluate a subset of the simulators in one call, with an optional Jacobian. They are not an extension API: new sequence families are Simulator subclasses. The classes are preferred when a sequence is reused, bound to a protocol, or passed to an estimator, reconstruction or design object.

Closed-form wrappers#

blochsim.bssfp_sim

Simulate the balanced SSFP steady-state signal.

blochsim.spgr_sim

Simulate the spoiled gradient-echo steady-state signal.

State-machine wrappers#

blochsim.fse_sim

Simulate a fast spin echo train.

blochsim.mprage_sim

Simulate the sampled MPRAGE shot in closed form.

blochsim.mp2rage_sim

Simulate the two MP2RAGE blocks in closed form.

blochsim.mpnrage_sim

Simulate an MPnRAGE train, every shot read.

blochsim.mrf_sim

Simulate an unbalanced SSFP MR fingerprinting train.
