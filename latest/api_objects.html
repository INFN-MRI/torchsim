API object index

API object index#

The stub page of every documented object. The API pages carry the same object lists as tables, each entry linking the stub this page writes; writing them here keeps them out of the toctree the sidebar is built from.

Estimator

Base class of the mapping methods.

DictionaryMatcher

Match signals to dictionary atoms by normalized complex inner product.

LookupTable

Read one unknown back from a monotonic signal curve.

NonlinearLeastSquares

Per-voxel Levenberg-Marquardt fit of the signal model.

PERK

Parameter estimation via regression with kernels (PERK).

execution

Set where per-voxel work runs inside the block.

offload

Stream host-resident volumes through CUDA devices inside the block.

utils.b1rms

Root-mean-square B1 of an RF pulse, in T.

Simulator

Base class of a pulse sequence protocol and its signal model.

SpinPhysics

The tissue properties a model exposes and the operators that realize its events.

EventOperators

Operator factories, one per kind of event.

Bounded

An initial value and the interval a designed parameter stays in.

SequenceDesign

A cost, the parameters it is minimized over, and their limits.

crlb

Cramer-Rao lower bound on the variance of each parameter.

ModelOperator

A signal model over parameter maps, with its derivatives.

GaussNewton

Solve a nonlinear inverse problem by repeated linearization.

Schedule

One damping for the whole volume, lowered geometrically.

TrustRegion

Levenberg-Marquardt: per-voxel damping, raised where a step does not pay.

direct

Solve every voxel's damped linearized system by batched least squares.

iterative

Build an inner solve from a regularized least-squares routine.

Subspace

Orthonormal temporal basis of a set of signals, for subspace reconstruction.

Operator

One module of a sequence: the events it plays and the time it spans.

Excitation

Excitation pulse.

Refocusing

Refocusing pulse, between crusher gradients by default.

Inversion

Ideal inversion pulse.

Saturation

Off-resonance saturation pulse that deposits power in a semisolid pool.

SampledPulse

Shaped pulse played as one hard pulse per sample, with free evolution between.

Readout

One ADC sample of the transverse magnetization.

Delay

Delay, optionally carrying a gradient action.

Dephase

Unbalanced gradient of zero duration.

Spoil

Ideal transverse spoiling of zero duration.

bSSFPReadout

Balanced SSFP readout: a sample, then the rest of the repetition time.

SSFPFidReadout

Unbalanced SSFP readout (FISP): a sample, then one unbalanced gradient.

SSFPEchoReadout

Reversed SSFP readout (PSIF): one unbalanced gradient, then a sample.

SPGRReadout

Spoiled gradient-echo readout: a sample, then ideal transverse spoiling.

FSEReadout

Fast spin-echo readout: the sample at a spin echo, marked as the echo centre.

rf_definition

Return the RF definition of a pulse envelope.

ShimDefinition

Transmit shim: a complex weight per channel for static parallel transmit.

compose_spinor

Return the Cayley-Klein pair of a shaped pulse, composed over its samples.

SequenceDescription

Event stream and RF resources of one repetition of a sequence.

read_mrd_description

Read an MRD stream up to its sequence description.

simulators.bSSFPSimulator

Balanced steady-state free precession (bSSFP) signal at the echo time.

simulators.SPGRSimulator

Spoiled gradient echo (SPGR, FLASH) steady state, in closed form.

simulators.MP2RAGESimulator

MP2RAGE: an inversion followed by two spoiled gradient-echo blocks.

simulators.InversionRecoverySimulator

Inversion-recovery T1 relaxometry, in closed form.

simulators.MultiEchoSimulator

Multi-echo T2 relaxometry, in closed form.

simulators.DoubleAngleSimulator

Double-angle B1 mapping, in closed form.

simulators.SpinEchoSimulator

Spin echo with partial longitudinal recovery, in closed form.

simulators.LookLockerSimulator

Look-Locker inversion recovery under a spoiled train of small flip angles.

simulators.MOLLISimulator

Modified Look-Locker inversion recovery (MOLLI): readout blocks separated by free recovery.

simulators.IRbSSFPSimulator

Inversion-recovery balanced SSFP (IR-TrueFISP) signal, in closed form.

simulators.HSFPSimulator

Hybrid-state free precession (HSFP) of a balanced train, in closed form.

simulators.MultiGradientEchoSimulator

Multi-gradient-echo signal of water and fat, in closed form.

simulators.IRMultiGradientEchoSimulator

Inversion-recovery multi-gradient-echo signal of water and fat, in closed form.

simulators.DiffusionSimulator

Diffusion-weighted signal attenuation by Gaussian diffusion, in closed form.

simulators.LorentzianSimulator

Z-spectrum (CEST) as equilibrium minus one Lorentzian line per pool.

simulators.ASLSimulator

Arterial spin labelling (ASL) control-label difference signal, in closed form.

simulators.FSESimulator

Fast (turbo) spin echo (FSE, RARE) train, sampled at every echo.

simulators.HyperechoSimulator

Hyperecho train: refocusing pulses mirrored about a central pulse.

simulators.StimulatedEchoSimulator

Stimulated-echo sequence: three pulses, sampled at the spin echo and the stimulated echo.

simulators.MPRAGESimulator

MPRAGE: an inversion followed by a spoiled gradient-echo train.

simulators.MPnRAGESimulator

MPnRAGE: an inversion and a spoiled gradient-echo train, every shot read.

simulators.MRFSimulator

MR fingerprinting with an unbalanced SSFP (FISP) train.

simulators.FLASHSimulator

Spoiled gradient-echo (FLASH, SPGR) train from equilibrium, optionally inverted first.

simulators.TrueFISPSimulator

Balanced SSFP (TrueFISP, FIESTA) train from equilibrium, optionally inverted first.

simulators.fmSSFPSimulator

Frequency-modulated balanced SSFP (fmSSFP) train.

simulators.CESTSimulator

Chemical exchange saturation transfer (CEST) Z-spectrum by Bloch-McConnell simulation.

bssfp_sim

Simulate the balanced SSFP steady-state signal.

spgr_sim

Simulate the spoiled gradient-echo steady-state signal.

fse_sim

Simulate a fast spin echo train.

mprage_sim

Simulate the sampled MPRAGE shot in closed form.

mp2rage_sim

Simulate the two MP2RAGE blocks in closed form.

mpnrage_sim

Simulate an MPnRAGE train, every shot read.

mrf_sim

Simulate an unbalanced SSFP MR fingerprinting train.
